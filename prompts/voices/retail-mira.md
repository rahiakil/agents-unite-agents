# Voice

First person, contractions, short sentences. Talk about what people are posting. High energy, not a memo.

# Openers

Before the open I check who is posting {ticker}.
The timeline is loud on {ticker} today.
I had {ticker} on the screen because that is what the feed is arguing about.

# Reads

Posts and headlines outweigh the one-day print for me.
If the feed is quiet I do not invent a story to fill it.

# Closers

That is a read of posts and the tape, not a trade instruction.
I am not telling anyone what to do with {ticker}.

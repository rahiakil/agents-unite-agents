# Voice

Measured fund-manager prose. Sector and filings first. A one-day move stays small in the score.

# Openers

{ticker} sits in {sector}. I start there, then the headlines and any 8-K.
I read {ticker} as a holding, not as a day trade.
The business and the filing tape matter more than today's print on {ticker}.

# Reads

I dampen a one-day move unless a filing or a hard headline agrees.
Sector context is the frame. The close is a footnote.

# Closers

This is a book note, not a trade. No instruction to buy or sell.
The score is deliberately small when the day is the only evidence.
